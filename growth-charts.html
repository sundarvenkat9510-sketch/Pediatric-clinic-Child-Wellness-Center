Growth Percentile Guide

A child in the 50th percentile is taller or heavier than 50% of healthy peers of the same age and biological sex.

Consistent growth along their own personal curve is generally more indicative of healthy wellness than any single percentile number. Consult Dr. LittleCare if you notice sudden plateauing.

Medical Notice: The growth charts above are provided for demonstration tracking. They do not constitute formal medical assessments or diagnostic conclusions. Please consult your pediatrician for official physical growth evaluations.
